What this scanner checks

Checks direct counterparties against 19 Tornado Cash classic pools and one router on Ethereum. It does not trace multiple hops, identify people, cover other chains, or include every historical deployment or Tornado Nova.

Incoming transfers do not establish intent. Failed transactions are attempts, not completed interactions. Internal call values can reflect call context (including delegatecalls) rather than an ETH transfer. Token symbols are unverified explorer metadata. Each transaction may appear in more than one feed.

Results cover the selected window within each feed's record cap, subject to explorer indexing. “No direct matches” is not proof of no past or indirect connection. This tool does not determine wrongdoing.

Registry checked October 12, 2026 against the Tornado Cash configuration. Balances are current explorer balances, not balances at the end of the lookback period.
